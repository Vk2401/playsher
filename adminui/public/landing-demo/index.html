Is Playsher free to use?+

Yes. The app is free to download and browsing grounds costs nothing. You only pay for the slot you book.

Do I have to pay the full amount online?+

No. You can hold the slot with a small advance online and settle the balance at the ground, or pay in full online. The split is shown before you confirm.

Can I cancel a booking?+

Cancel from My Bookings and the slot is released straight away for other players. Refunds follow the cancellation policy shown on the booking screen.

I do not have a full team. Can I still play?+

Host an open game with the spots you need filled, or join one nearby that is short of players.

How do I sign in?+

With your mobile number and a one-time password. There is no password to remember.

I own a ground. How do I list it?+

Apply on the for ground owners page. Once you are approved you get a panel for slots, bookings, cash at the gate and settlements.
