Owner questions.

What does it cost to list?+

Nothing to list. Commercial terms are agreed with you before your ground goes live.

Can I still take walk-ins?+

Yes. Block the slot in the panel and it stops being bookable in the app straight away.

How do I get paid?+

Money paid online is settled to your registered bank account; money collected at the gate you keep. The Earnings screen shows both sides.

Can I list more than one ground?+

Yes. One login handles all of them, and every list can be filtered by ground.
